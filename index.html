ABOUT PETCHI P

Power Platform Developer building robust enterprise applications with precision.

I am a Power Platform Developer with 3+ years of experience architecting, developing, and deploying business applications using Microsoft Power Apps, Power Automate, and SharePoint Online at Veelead Solutions.
